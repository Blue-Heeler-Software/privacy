PrismShare Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-16

PrismShare moves files and live audio between a screen and a camera as colour codes. It is built so that a privacy policy has almost nothing to describe.

The app does not hold network permission. It is incapable of sending anything, to us or to anyone.

What the app collects

Nothing. PrismShare collects no personal data, no usage data, no analytics, no identifiers, and no diagnostics.

What the app transmits

Nothing. The app does not request network permission, so it cannot send anything off your device. Files you receive are decoded from the camera image and stored only where you save them. Audio you receive is played and not retained. The camera image itself is processed on the device and never leaves it.

Permissions

  • Camera: used to read PS Codes from screens. Frames are decoded in memory on the device. The app takes no photographs and records no video.

That is the only permission the app requests.

Third parties

There are none. No SDK in the app communicates with any service, because the app cannot communicate at all.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If a future version of the app gains any capability that touches data or the network, this policy will describe it before that version ships, and its effective date will change.
